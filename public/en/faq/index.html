Frequently asked questions

Short answers to what couples ask me most.

How much does wedding photography cost?

I don't do fixed packages. A full wedding starts at around 1,000 EUR, and the final quote depends on hours, location and what you'd like. Send me your date and place and I'll send a tailored quote.

What isn't included?

Printed albums, extra sessions (after-wedding, Trash The Dress) and a second photographer are added separately.

Do you travel outside Brașov?

Yes, I'll go wherever you want. For events outside Brașov, travel and accommodation are added.

We're shy in front of the camera. Is that a problem?

Not at all. My style is documentary: you don't need to pose. Most couples forget I'm there after the first few minutes.

When do we get the photos?

(To confirm with Dan) Delivery time and approximate number of photos.

How do we book a date?

Message me on WhatsApp or email with your date and place. We'll have a quick chat, then confirm the date.
